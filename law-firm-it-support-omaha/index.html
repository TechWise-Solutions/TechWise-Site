Plain English

Your ethical duties, covered on the technical side

The ABA Model Rules ask lawyers to keep up with the benefits and risks of relevant technology (Rule 1.1, Comment 8) and to make reasonable efforts to prevent unauthorized access to client information (Rule 1.6(c)).

"Reasonable efforts" in practice means things like multi-factor sign-in, encrypted devices, tested backups and a plan for what happens if something goes wrong. Our free assessment shows where your firm stands on each one, in writing.

We don't give legal advice about your obligations. We make the technical side something you can point to.
